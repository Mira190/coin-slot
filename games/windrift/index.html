    • 漂移按住 Shift＋方向，火花由白→蓝→橙→紫，越久越强，同时集气。Drift: sparks change colour as the charge grows.
    • 小喷松开 Shift，车身回正时 喷 灯亮 0.45 秒，此时点 ↑；0.15 秒内为「完美」。Micro boost: tap ↑ while the light is on.
    • 双喷漂移中反打方向拉正车头（仍按住 Shift）→ 第一次亮灯点 ↑，再松 Shift → 第二次亮灯再点 ↑。Double: pull the nose straight mid-drift, tap, release, tap again.
    • 断位漂移松开 Shift 后 0.5 秒内同向再按 Shift，车头再甩一下继续漂，接上小喷即断位双喷。Broken drift: re-press Shift within 0.5 s.
    • 点漂轻点 Shift（不足 0.3 秒）快速调整车头。Tap drift: a quick flick to set the line.
    • 甩尾漂移先按住 Shift，再连点两下同一方向（0.4 秒内），大弧度甩尾过长弯。Tail whip: hold Shift, then double-tap the direction.
    • 空喷 · 落地喷腾空时点 ↑ 空喷；落地 0.35 秒内再点 ↑ 落地喷。Air boost in the air, landing boost on touchdown.
    • 起步喷倒计时 GO 前 0.3 秒内按下 ↑。Rocket start: press ↑ just before GO.
    • 氮气 · 集气集气条满得一瓶氮气（最多 2 瓶）；小喷与氮气叠加有 CW / WCW / CWW 连喷。Full gauge = 1 bottle (max 2). Stack boosts for CW / WCW / CWW.
    键位提示 Keyboard note: 方向键是主键位（WASD 也可以，但比赛中 Ctrl＋W 会关闭浏览器标签页，浏览器不允许网页拦截——用 WASD 时请用 Space 放氮气）。比赛中其他 Ctrl 组合键已被拦截。macOS 上 Ctrl＋方向键可能切换桌面：点按 Ctrl，不要按住。
    Arrow keys are the primary layout. WASD also works, but Ctrl+W closes the tab and browsers don't let pages block it, so use Space for nitro with WASD. Other Ctrl combos are blocked while racing. On macOS, Ctrl+arrow can switch desktops: tap Ctrl, don't hold it.
    触屏：自动油门，◀ ▶ 转向，漂移 / 喷 / 氮气按钮。Touch: auto-throttle, ◀ ▶ steer, Drift / Boost / Nitro buttons.
    手柄：摇杆转向，A/RT 油门（点 A 放喷），B/LT 刹车，LB/RB 漂移，X 氮气，Y 道具2，Back 视角，Start 暂停。Gamepad: stick steer, A/RT throttle (tap A to boost), B/LT brake, LB/RB drift, X nitro, Y item 2, Back camera, Start pause.
